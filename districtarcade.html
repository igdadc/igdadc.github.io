District Arcade 2026


District Arcade is a celebration of games and game makers from Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, D.C. Come out to this free and family-friendly event held in partnership with the D.C. Public Library. 30+ game developers will be showcasing their games for anyone to play. Held at the Metro accessible Martin Luther King Jr. library in downtown D.C., we welcome anyone interested in game development (and playing games!) to join the fun!

🕹️ RSVP and get event announcements via our Meetup page for this event.

We look forward to seeing you there!

Event Information
  • Location: Lab - Bottom floor of the MLK Library
  • Address: 901 G St NW, Washington DC, 20001
  • Date and Time: November 14th 2026 1:00-5:00 PM
  • Meetup Page: https://www.meetup.com/igda-dc/events/316590872
